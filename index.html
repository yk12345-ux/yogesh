Our Story

Built from Hadapsar,
for businesses everywhere

I started Axinex Technologies in 2023 with one clear idea — that businesses in Pune deserve the same quality of digital solutions that big corporations get, without the corporate price tag or the runaround. My uncle, who ran a small manufacturing unit in Hadapsar, struggled for years with manual tracking and outdated paper-based processes. Watching him lose time and money every single day was the push I needed to build something that actually works for businesses like his.

We are not a vendor — we are your technology partner. When you bring us a problem, we sit with you, understand your workflow, and build something tailored. We don't sell you a template and disappear. Every project at Axinex goes through proper documentation, structured QA, and post-delivery support — the same standards that enterprise software demands.

Two years in, we've served private businesses, educational institutions, and government projects. Our office is right here in Sanket Park, Hadapsar — come visit us, have a chai, and let's talk about what you want to build. We respond to every enquiry within two hours, and we give you an honest scope and estimate — no fluff.
